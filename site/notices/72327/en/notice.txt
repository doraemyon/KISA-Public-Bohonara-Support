Title:
HPE Product Security Update Advisory

Summary:
Today, we will spread the contents of the KISA notice "HPE Product Security Update Advisory", and we advise those in charge of the affected versions to upgrade to the newest version.

Body:
□ Overview
o HPE has released security updates to address vulnerabilities in its products [1]
o Users running affected versions are advised to update to the latest version according to the mitigation measures

□ Description
o Vulnerabilities occurring in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5) and Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6) (CVE-2026-65204) [1]
o Vulnerabilities occurring in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7) (CVE-2026-65205) [1]
o Vulnerabilities occurring in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7) (CVE-2026-65206) [1]
o Vulnerabilities occurring in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7) (CVE-2026-65207) [1]
o Vulnerabilities occurring in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7) (CVE-2026-65209) [1]
